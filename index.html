about

Ash Howell (they/them) is a speculative fiction writer living on the shores of Lake Michigan. Their work has appeared in Lightspeed, Baffling Magazine, Astrolabe, and elsewhere. They are a 2023 alum of Viable Paradise, a software engineer, and parent of two voracious bookworms.

They are represented by Eric Showers at Howard Morhaim Literary Agency.
